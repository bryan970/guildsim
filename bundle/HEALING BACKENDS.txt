GuildSim healing backend — 24 November 2026

Discipline now runs native healing fights using WoWSims Forever v1.0.7
spell, talent, racial, mana and item mechanics, plus GuildSim's own declared
friendly-damage assignments and casting policy. Source is included.

Metrics: effective healing, actually consumed absorbs, useful HPS,
overhealing, assignment survival, mana reserve and sampling margins.
Group, tank and burst assignments have separate cached fight records.
Gear catalogs use stable reference trinkets; pair comparisons test both
orders. Broad catalogs screen variants and refine leading candidates.

The initialization hook is an additive MIT-licensed host extension.
Existing native spell implementations and Ret entry points are preserved.

KFC Helper was reviewed as a reference for useful-healing metrics:
https://kfcguild.online/tools/forever/tier-list/healers
No KFC code, service, account or runtime is integrated or redistributed.

Limits: stationary post-mitigation assignments, fixed healing priorities,
no movement, dispels or competition from other healers, and no full-raid
prediction. Native item-effect gaps remain labelled. In-game guild
acceptance and fight calibration are still required. See README.md for
exact default damage, buffs, consumables and casting-policy assumptions.
